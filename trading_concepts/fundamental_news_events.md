# Macro-Economics & High-Impact News: The Fundamental Rulebook

While Technical Analysis (FVGs, Liquidity Sweeps, Order Blocks) tells us *where* the market will react, Fundamental Analysis (Economic News) provides the *catalyst* that drives price into those technical levels violently.

**CRITICAL RULE:** Do NOT trade a standard technical setup 15 minutes before high-impact US news. The market will often consolidate (build liquidity) just before the news, and then violently sweep both sides when the news hits.

Here is the exact playbook for interpreting major US Economic events.

---

## 1. The Federal Reserve (FED) & FOMC
The Federal Reserve is the Central Bank of the United States. The FOMC (Federal Open Market Committee) is the group within the FED that decides on US Interest Rates.
* **The Goal:** The FED raises interest rates to fight inflation, and cuts interest rates to boost a weak economy.
* **Interest Rate Decision:** The actual number released (e.g., 5.25%).
* **FOMC Press Conference:** 30 minutes after the rate decision, the Fed Chairman (Jerome Powell) speaks. **This causes more volatility than the rate itself.** The market hangs on his every word to predict *future* rate moves (Hawkish vs. Dovish).

### The Market Impact (The Golden Rule of Rates)
* **Hawkish (Raising Rates or planning to keep them high):**
  * **USD:** Goes UP 📈 (Higher yield attracts foreign capital)
  * **Gold (XAUUSD) / Silver (XAGUSD):** Goes DOWN 📉 (Gold yields 0%, so high cash rates make Gold less attractive)
  * **US Stocks/Indices (SPX, NAS100):** Goes DOWN 📉 (Borrowing money becomes expensive for companies)
* **Dovish (Cutting Rates or planning to cut them):**
  * **USD:** Goes DOWN 📉
  * **Gold / Silver:** Goes UP 📈
  * **US Stocks/Indices:** Goes UP 📈

---

## 2. CPI (Consumer Price Index) & PPI (Producer Price Index)
CPI measures **Inflation** (the cost of goods for consumers). PPI measures inflation for producers (wholesale costs). If CPI is high, it means inflation is rising.

* **Why it matters:** The FED's main job is to keep inflation around 2%. If CPI comes in hotter (higher) than expected, the market assumes the FED will have to raise interest rates (Hawkish).

### The Market Impact
* **CPI HIGHER than Expected (Hot Inflation):**
  * **Result:** Market prices in FED rate hikes.
  * **USD:** Goes UP 📈
  * **Gold (XAUUSD):** Goes DOWN 📉 (Crushed by the rising dollar)
* **CPI LOWER than Expected (Cooling Inflation):**
  * **Result:** Market prices in FED rate cuts.
  * **USD:** Goes DOWN 📉
  * **Gold (XAUUSD):** Goes UP 📈 (Rocket fuel for Gold)

---

## 3. NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls) & Unemployment Rate
Released on the first Friday of every month. It measures how many new jobs were created in the US (excluding farms). The FED watches this closely because a too-hot job market causes inflation, while a crashing job market forces them to cut rates to save the economy.

### The Market Impact
* **NFP HIGHER than Expected (Strong Economy/Jobs):**
  * **Result:** Economy is too hot, FED might keep rates high.
  * **USD:** Goes UP 📈
  * **Gold (XAUUSD):** Goes DOWN 📉
* **NFP LOWER than Expected (Weak Economy/Jobs):**
  * **Result:** Economy is cracking, FED will have to cut rates to save it.
  * **USD:** Goes DOWN 📉
  * **Gold (XAUUSD):** Goes UP 📈

---

## How to Synthesize News & Technicals

When your `WebSearchAgent` provides news headlines, analyze them using this rulebook before making a trading decision.

### Example Scenario:
**Live News Sentiment:** "US CPI comes in at 3.5%, significantly hotter than the 3.1% expectation."
**Technical Signal:** "SMC Bullish Order Block forming on XAUUSD (Gold)."

**The Brain's Decision:** **HOLD (or Veto the Buy)**
**Reasoning:** Even though a Bullish technical setup is forming on Gold, the HOT CPI data means the US Dollar will skyrocket, which will crush Gold. The technical Order Block will likely fail and become a Liquidity Sweep. Do not buy Gold against a hot CPI print.
