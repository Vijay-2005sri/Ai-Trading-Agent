## DETAILED CONCEPT-TO-CONCEPT RELATIONSHIPS

### 1. LIQUIDITY ↔ MARKET STRUCTURE

**Why they are connected:**
Market structure (swing highs and lows) creates the levels where liquidity accumulates. Every swing high has BSL above it (stops from shorts). Every swing low has SSL below it (stops from longs).

**How they interact:**
- Swing highs define where BSL sits → price is drawn to these levels to sweep them
- Swing lows define where SSL sits → price is drawn to these levels to sweep them
- When a structural level is broken (BOS), it often involves sweeping the liquidity at that level
- When structure shifts (CHoCH/MSS), it often begins with a liquidity sweep at the trend extreme

**Practical implication:**
The agent should always identify BOTH the structural swing points AND the liquidity at those points. They are two views of the same thing.

---

### 2. LIQUIDITY ↔ ORDER BLOCKS

**Why they are connected:**
Order Blocks form AFTER liquidity is swept. The liquidity sweep provides the volume (triggered stops) that institutions use to fill their OB orders. Without a liquidity event, an OB is less meaningful.

**How they interact:**
- Liquidity sweep creates the conditions for institutional order placement
- The OB is the zone where those orders were placed
- The FVG/displacement that follows the OB is the evidence of those orders being executed
- When price returns to the OB, it may sweep any minor liquidity (inducement) before reaching the actual OB zone

**Practical implication:**
An OB that was preceded by a clear liquidity sweep is significantly stronger than one without. Always check: "Was liquidity taken before this OB formed?"

---

### 3. LIQUIDITY ↔ FVG

**Why they are connected:**
An FVG is the footprint left by the aggressive displacement move that follows a liquidity sweep. The FVG exists BECAUSE institutional orders (funded by the swept liquidity) created a one-sided move so fast that a gap formed.

**How they interact:**
- Liquidity is swept → institutions enter → displacement occurs → FVG forms
- The FVG marks where the imbalance occurred
- Price may return to the FVG to rebalance, providing an entry opportunity
- If there was no liquidity sweep before the FVG → the FVG is less reliable

---

### 4. LIQUIDITY SWEEP ↔ MSS (Market Structure Shift)

**Why they are connected:**
An MSS is often initiated by a liquidity sweep. The sweep triggers stops, provides liquidity for institutional entry, and the resulting displacement causes the structural shift.

**How they interact:**
- Price sweeps a liquidity level (e.g., takes out the swing high in an uptrend)
- The stops triggered provide volume for institutional selling
- Aggressive selling (displacement) follows
- The displacement breaks below the most recent HL → CHoCH
- If the displacement is strong (FVG created, OB identified) → MSS confirmed
- The MSS signals a trend reversal

**Practical implication:**
When a sweep occurs at a trend extreme → immediately watch for displacement and CHoCH/MSS. This is the most high-probability reversal setup.

---

### 5. DISPLACEMENT ↔ FVG

**Why they are connected:**
Displacement CREATES FVGs. Without displacement, there is no FVG. They are cause and effect.

**How they interact:**
- A displacement candle moves so aggressively that the gap between candle 1's range and candle 3's range is not overlapping → FVG
- The strength of the displacement determines the size and significance of the FVG
- A weak displacement produces a tiny FVG (less meaningful)
- A strong displacement produces a large FVG (more meaningful)

---

### 6. DISPLACEMENT ↔ ORDER BLOCK

**Why they are connected:**
Displacement validates an Order Block. The OB is the origin candle; the displacement is the evidence that institutional orders at that origin were executed.

**How they interact:**
- OB = the last opposing candle
- Displacement = the strong move away from the OB
- Without displacement → the "OB" is just a random candle
- The displacement proves that the OB zone was indeed where institutional orders entered

---

### 7. BOS ↔ CONTINUATION

**Why they are connected:**
BOS is the structural signal for continuation. When a BOS occurs, the trend is confirmed as ongoing.

**How they interact:**
- BOS = price breaks a swing point in the direction of the existing trend
- This confirms the trend is alive
- After BOS → look for the FVG/OB created by the move → enter on pullback
- BOS is the agent's signal to continue looking for trend-direction entries

---

### 8. CHoCH/MSS ↔ POTENTIAL REVERSAL

**Why they are connected:**
CHoCH and MSS are the structural signals for potential reversals.

**How they interact:**
- CHoCH = first break against the trend → warning
- MSS = CHoCH + displacement + sweep → confirmed shift
- After MSS → the directional bias changes
- Look for entries in the new direction on pullbacks to the FVG/OB created by the MSS move

---

### 9. OB ↔ FVG

**Why they are connected:**
They are created by the same event (displacement) and often appear together or adjacent.

**How they interact:**
- The OB is at the ORIGIN of the displacement (the last opposing candle)
- The FVG is IN the displacement move (the gap between candles)
- They may overlap, be adjacent, or be separated by a few candles
- When they overlap → very high-confluence zone
- Both serve as potential entry zones → the agent should identify both and prioritize the overlap

---

### 10. OB ↔ PREMIUM/DISCOUNT

**Why they are connected:**
An OB's strength depends on where it is relative to the dealing range.

**How they interact:**
- Bullish OB in discount = STRONG → price is cheap + institutional buy zone
- Bullish OB in premium = WEAK → price is expensive for buys
- Bearish OB in premium = STRONG → price is expensive + institutional sell zone
- Bearish OB in discount = WEAK → price is cheap for sells

---

### 11. SUPPLY/DEMAND ↔ ORDER BLOCKS

**Why they are connected:**
They describe similar phenomena through different frameworks.

**How they interact:**
- S/D zones are broader areas identified by base patterns (RBR, DBD, etc.)
- OBs are more precise zones identified by specific candle + displacement rules
- An OB is often found WITHIN a S/D zone
- When both overlap → strong confluence
- The agent uses OBs as primary, S/D as supplementary confirmation

---

### 12. SESSION TIMING ↔ LIQUIDITY

**Why they are connected:**
Liquidity events (sweeps, stop hunts) occur more frequently during specific sessions and Kill Zones.

**How they interact:**
- Asian session → builds liquidity (range forms with BSL above and SSL below)
- London Kill Zone → often sweeps one end of the Asian range (manipulation)
- NY Kill Zone → continues or completes the directional move (expansion)
- Kill Zones are when the highest-probability setups trigger

---

### 13. ACCUMULATION ↔ MANIPULATION ↔ DISTRIBUTION

**Why they are connected:**
They are the three phases of the AMD / Power of Three cycle.

**How they interact:**
- Accumulation = range builds liquidity on both sides
- Manipulation = one side's liquidity is swept (the fake move)
- Distribution = the real move in the opposite direction (expansion)
- This cycle repeats on every timeframe and every session

---

### 14. RISK MANAGEMENT ↔ ENTRY MODEL

**Why they are connected:**
A technically valid setup is worthless if the risk parameters don't work.

**How they interact:**
- Entry zone (OB/FVG) determines the entry price
- Invalidation level determines SL placement
- Next liquidity target determines TP placement
- SL and TP determine R:R → must be ≥ 1.5
- R:R and account risk % determine position size
- If any of these fail the risk criteria → the trade is rejected regardless of how perfect the setup looks

---

### 15. INDUCEMENT ↔ ORDER BLOCKS / LIQUIDITY

**Why they are connected:**
Inducement is the trap layer above the real OB. It sweeps minor liquidity before price reaches the actual entry zone.

**How they interact:**
- Minor swing points form within a pullback → these hold inducement liquidity
- Price sweeps the inducement (takes minor stops) → this is NOT the real entry
- After sweeping inducement → price reaches the actual OB/FVG → THIS is the entry
- The agent must recognize inducement to avoid entering too early
